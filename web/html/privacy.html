01

Information we collect.

We collect only the information necessary to operate the DevGuild DG community, deliver our programs, and maintain a safe environment for every member. We do not sell personal data to third parties, and we never will.

Information you provide directly

When you join DevGuild DG, register for a session, submit a project, or contact us, you may provide:

  • Identity information — your full name or chosen display name, used to identify you within the community and on project teams.
  • Email address — used for account creation, membership verification, event invitations, and important community updates. We require a valid email to prevent duplicate and fraudulent accounts.
  • Profile details — an optional bio, profile photo, social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, personal website), and skill tags you choose to display on your community profile.
  • Membership applications — information you submit during the intake process, including your learning goals, technical background, and the certificates or badges you complete as part of our membership requirements.
  • Project contributions — code repositories, design files, documentation, and other deliverables you create or share through Guild projects, build nights, or showcases.
  • Communications — messages you send through our contact forms, email correspondence, feedback surveys, and any content you post in community channels (Telegram, Discord, or other platforms we use).

Information collected automatically

When you visit the DevGuild DG website, our servers may automatically record:

  • Usage data — the pages you visit, the order in which you navigate, time spent on each page, scroll depth, and general interaction patterns. This data is aggregated and anonymized — it helps us understand which sections of the site are most useful and where the experience can be improved.
  • Device and browser data — your browser type and version, operating system, screen resolution, and device type (desktop, tablet, mobile). We use this solely to ensure the site renders correctly and to diagnose layout issues across devices.
  • Referrer information — the URL or search engine that brought you to our site, if available. This helps us understand how people discover DevGuild DG but is never tied to your identity in our analytics.
  • IP address — recorded in server access logs for security monitoring, abuse prevention, and geographic aggregate analytics (e.g., "40% of visitors are from Southeast Asia"). IP addresses are not used to identify or track individual users and are rotated from logs every 90 days.

What we do not collect

We want to be transparent about what we deliberately avoid collecting:

  • Full legal names or government-issued identification (unless required for a specific partnered event, in which case we ask separately and with clear consent).
  • Financial information — we do not process payments, and we do not store credit card numbers or banking details.
  • Biometric data, precise geolocation, or health information.
  • Browsing history outside of our own website.
02

How we use your information.

Every piece of data we collect is tied to a specific, limited purpose. We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to what is described below.

Operating the community

  • Account management — creating and maintaining your member profile, tracking your membership tier (Apprentice, Developer, Senior Developer), and managing your participation in programs like Foundations, Build Nights, and Guild Projects.
  • Event coordination — sending session invitations, RSVP confirmations, schedule reminders, and post-event follow-ups for workshops, hackathons (such as AQUANONS, TABANG, and Handa 360), and community meetups.
  • Mentorship matching — pairing members with mentors based on stated learning goals, skill tags, and availability. Matching data is handled by council members and is not shared outside the organization.

Communication

  • Community updates — newsletters, project announcements, call-for-contributors posts, and important organizational changes delivered via email or community channels you are subscribed to.
  • Direct correspondence — responses to your inquiries sent through the contact form, email, or social media messages. We retain these threads to provide continuity if you follow up later.
  • Feedback and surveys — optional satisfaction surveys and retrospective forms shared after events. Participation is voluntary, and survey responses are anonymized before being used for program improvement.

Improvement and security

  • Site analytics — aggregate usage data (page views, session duration, device types) is reviewed monthly to identify broken links, underperforming pages, and accessibility gaps. No individual user is singled out in these reports.
  • Security monitoring — IP addresses and access logs are reviewed to detect and block brute-force attacks, spam submissions, and unauthorized access attempts. We use rate limiting and firewall rules — not behavioral profiling — to protect the site.
  • Legal compliance — when required by applicable law or valid legal process, we may retain or disclose specific data to fulfill a legal obligation (e.g., responding to a court order or government request).

We will never use your information for automated decision-making, behavioral profiling, targeted advertising, or any purpose that conflicts with our mission of ethical, transparent technology practice.

03

Cookies and local storage.

The DevGuild DG website is intentionally lightweight. We use minimal browser storage and do not rely on third-party cookies or tracking scripts.

What we store

  • Theme preference — your dark/light mode choice is saved in localStorage under the key devguild-theme. This value never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. It allows the site to remember your visual preference on return visits.
  • Mobile menu state — a temporary flag in localStorage that tracks whether the mobile navigation menu was open during your last visit. This improves the experience on smaller screens and is overwritten on each page load.

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or conversion trackers.
  • No third-party analytics scripts (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.).
  • No social media tracking widgets or embedded marketing tags.
  • No fingerprinting techniques or canvas-based identification.

If we ever introduce a cookie for a new feature (e.g., a session-based tool), we will update this section and request your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.

04

When we share information.

DevGuild DG does not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Your information is shared only in the following narrow circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent — when you ask us to introduce you to a partner organization, sponsor, or external mentor, we share only the information you approve (typically your name, email, and project summary). We confirm the scope of sharing before anything is sent.
  • Service providers we rely on — we use a small number of trusted tools to operate the website and community. These may include our hosting provider, email delivery service, and cloud storage for project files. Each provider is contractually bound to use your data only for the service they provide to us and is prohibited from using it for their own purposes. Current providers include:
    • Static site hosting (serves the website files you see in your browser).
    • Email delivery (sends membership confirmations and event invitations you have opted into).
    • Cloud storage (stores project files and documentation shared by members during active Guild projects).
  • Legal obligations — if we are required by law, regulation, or a valid court order to disclose specific data, we will comply. When legally permitted, we will notify you before the disclosure so you have an opportunity to seek legal remedies.
  • Protection of rights — we may share information if we reasonably believe it is necessary to protect the safety of DevGuild DG members, the public, or the rights and property of the organization (e.g., in response to a credible threat or confirmed security incident).

We never share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone who would use it for marketing purposes. If a partnership requires data sharing beyond what is described here, we will ask for your informed consent first.

05

Data security.

We take a practical, layered approach to protecting your information. While no system is completely immune to risk, we apply the following measures:

Technical safeguards

  • HTTPS everywhere — all data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. This prevents interception during transit and is enforced on every page of the site.
  • Secure hosting — our website is hosted on infrastructure that provides DDoS protection, automated backups, and network-level firewalls. Server access is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • Encrypted storage — member data at rest (email addresses, profile details) is stored in encrypted databases. Encryption keys are managed separately from the data itself.
  • Access logging — all administrative access to member data is logged with timestamps, the acting account, and the action performed. Logs are reviewed monthly for anomalies.

Organizational safeguards

  • Principle of least privilege — council members and volunteers are granted access only to the data required for their role. A mentor does not have access to administrative tools; a project lead does not have access to member email lists unless their role requires it.
  • Confidentiality agreements — all council members and individuals with elevated access sign confidentiality agreements covering member data.
  • Incident response — we maintain an internal incident response procedure that includes containment, assessment, notification, and post-incident review. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours of confirming the breach, including what data was affected and what steps we are taking.

We conduct a security review of our tools and providers at least once per year, or whenever a significant change is made to how we handle data.

06

Data retention.

We do not keep your data indefinitely. Each category of information has a defined retention period aligned with its purpose:

  • Active membership data — your name, email, profile details, and participation records are retained for as long as your membership is active and you are participating in programs or contributing to projects.
  • Inactive accounts — if you have not logged in, attended a session, or interacted with the community for 12 consecutive months, your personal data is flagged for deletion. You will receive a notification email 30 days before deletion, giving you the option to reactivate. After 30 days with no response, your profile and associated personal data are permanently removed. Anonymized contribution records (e.g., "Member X contributed to Project Y") may be retained for historical documentation.
  • Contact form submissions — messages sent through the contact form are retained for up to 24 months to provide continuity in follow-up conversations. After 24 months, submissions are automatically purged.
  • Event attendance records — records of which sessions you attended are retained for 12 months after the event for internal reporting and to track your progress through membership tiers. After 12 months, attendance data is anonymized.
  • Server access logs — IP addresses and access timestamps from server logs are rotated and deleted every 90 days. Logs are used for security monitoring only and are never used to profile individual visitors.
  • Project deliverables — code, designs, and documentation you contribute to Guild projects are retained as part of the project's public or archived repository, consistent with the project's open-source license (if applicable). Your authorship is credited per your profile, but your email or other personal details are not embedded in the repository unless you choose to include them.

You may request immediate deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@devguild.dg. We will process your request within 14 business days and confirm once your data has been removed.

07

Your rights and choices.

You have meaningful control over your personal data. Regardless of where you are located, we respect the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can request a complete copy of all personal data we hold about you, including your profile information, membership history, event attendance, and any messages you have sent through our platforms. We will provide this in a readable, structured format (JSON or CSV) within 30 days of your request.
  • Right to correct — if any information we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you can ask us to update it. You can also edit your profile details directly through your account settings at any time.
  • Right to delete — you can request that we erase your personal data. We will comply unless we are legally required to retain specific records (e.g., financial records for tax purposes, though DevGuild DG does not currently process payments). We will confirm deletion within 14 business days.
  • Right to restrict processing — you can ask us to limit how we use your data while we address a dispute about its accuracy or if you object to a particular processing activity.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON, CSV, or similar) so you can transfer it to another service. This includes your profile, membership records, and any contributions you have made.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent (e.g., receiving newsletters), you can withdraw that consent at any time by unsubscribing through the link in any email or by contacting us directly. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the withdrawal.
  • Right to object — you can object to any processing that is not strictly necessary for the operation of the community. We will honor your objection unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@devguild.dg with the subject line "Privacy Request" and specify which right you would like to exercise. We will verify your identity before processing the request and respond within 30 days. There is no charge for exercising these rights, except in cases of manifestly unfounded or excessive requests, in which case we may charge a reasonable administrative fee.

08

Children's privacy.

DevGuild DG is a community for students and aspiring developers, but our website and programs are designed for individuals who are at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our systems. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@devguild.dg and we will investigate and remove the data promptly.

For members between the ages of 13 and 18, we encourage parental awareness of their minor's participation in the community. We do not require parental consent for minors over 13 to participate, but parents may contact us at any time to review, modify, or delete their child's data.

09

Third-party links.

The DevGuild DG website and community platforms may contain links to external services and platforms, including:

  • GitHub — where we host open-source project repositories, contribution guides, and member profiles. GitHub's own privacy policy governs any data you provide directly to them.
  • Telegram and Discord — community discussion channels where members interact in real time. Messages posted in these channels are subject to the platform's data handling practices.
  • Social media — Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp, and other platforms linked from our footer or event pages. Each has its own privacy policy.
  • External learning platforms — links to courses, documentation, or tools recommended in our programs (e.g., freeCodeCamp, MDN, Coursera). These are provided for educational convenience and are not operated by DevGuild DG.

When you click a link that takes you away from our website, we are no longer responsible for how that external site collects, uses, or protects your data. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing personal information.

If you find a link on our site that leads to a deceptive or harmful service, please report it to hello@devguild.dg so we can review and remove it.

10

Changes to this policy.

We may update this privacy policy as our practices evolve or as required by law. When we make changes:

  • Minor changes (typographical corrections, clarifications that do not alter your rights) — we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and publish the revised policy. No separate notification is required.
  • Material changes (new data collection practices, changes to how we use or share your data, new third-party providers) — we will provide at least 14 days' advance notice through our community channels (email, Telegram, and Discord) and through a prominent banner on the website. During this notice period, you may exercise your right to delete your data before the new policy takes effect.

We maintain a changelog of all significant updates at the bottom of this page. Your continued use of the website or participation in the community after a policy update takes effect means you accept the revised policy. If you do not agree with the changes, you are free to stop using the website and request deletion of your data.

Contact us.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, how we handle your data, or if you would like to exercise any of the rights described above, we are here to help.

Primary contact

  • Email: hello@devguild.dg — for all privacy-related inquiries, data requests, and general questions about this policy. Please include "Privacy Request" in the subject line so we can route your message to the right person.

Other channels

  • Contact page: devguild.dg/contact — use this form if you prefer not to send an email directly. Select "Privacy" as the subject so your message is handled with appropriate priority.
  • Community channels: you may also reach us on Telegram or Facebook Messenger (linked in the website footer). However, for requests involving personal data access, correction, or deletion, we recommend using email or the contact form so we can verify your identity and maintain a formal record of your request.

Response commitment

We aim to acknowledge all privacy-related inquiries within 3 business days and provide a substantive response within 30 business days. If your request is complex, we may need additional time — we will notify you if this is the case and explain the reason for the delay.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We encourage you to contact us first so we can attempt to resolve the matter directly.
